Privacy Policy

Pikko Games · Last updated 15 September 2026

This policy covers all Pikko Games mobile apps. It explains what the apps collect, why, and what you can do about it.

The short version

The games work offline and do not ask you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, location, or files. Your games, statistics and settings stay on your device.

The apps do show ads, and the ad provider (Google AdMob) uses an advertising identifier. That is the only data leaving your device.

What stays on your device

  • Saved games in progress
  • In the card games, the short deal code shown after each hand. It only reproduces the shuffle and contains nothing about you.
  • Scores, best results, win counts and daily streaks
  • Daily challenge history and trophies
  • Your language, difficulty and other in-game preferences

This is stored locally by the app. It is not uploaded anywhere, and deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

The apps display ads through Google AdMob. To do this, AdMob may access your device's advertising identifier and technical information such as device type, operating system version and coarse region.

DataPurposeShared with
Advertising ID Serving and measuring ads, limiting how often the same ad is shown Google AdMob
Device and app information Ad delivery and fraud prevention Google AdMob

Google's own handling of that data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Your choices about ads

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before serving personalised ads. You can change your answer at any time under Settings → Privacy settings in the app.
  • On any device, you can reset or delete your advertising ID from your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • You can remove ads entirely with the one-time in-app purchase. There is no subscription.

Purchases

If you buy the ad-free upgrade, the transaction is handled by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. The app only records that the purchase was made, on your device.

Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

Data retention and deletion

Because game data never leaves your device, uninstalling the app removes it completely. There is no server-side account to delete. For the advertising identifier, use the Android settings linked above.
